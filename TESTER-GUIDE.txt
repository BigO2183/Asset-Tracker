SIMPLESTOCK — TESTER GUIDE

Thanks for testing SimpleStock.

Please use it like you normally manage resale or estate-sale inventory.

Try these things:
1. Tap Try Demo.
2. Search for an item.
3. Change a status.
4. Open Item Details.
5. Edit/correct an item.
6. Mark an item sold.
7. Try Scan or manual code lookup.
8. Look at Sales and Reports.
9. Switch between Reseller and Estate Sale.
10. Tap Feedback and tell me what felt useful, confusing, unnecessary, or missing.

Please do NOT worry about being polite. Honest feedback is what helps.

The main questions:
- What felt useful?
- What confused or annoyed you?
- What would you remove?
- What is missing?
- Would you actually use this?

Demo changes are temporary and do not affect another tester's real workspace.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

SimpleStock stores workspace account information and inventory data to provide inventory, sales, reporting, and synchronization features. Workspace data is separated by workspace ID.

Passwords are stored as hashed values. SimpleStock does not intentionally store payment-card information in this version.

Workspace owners control the inventory and staff information they enter. Users should avoid storing unnecessary sensitive personal information in item notes.

Backups exported by users are downloaded to their own device and should be stored securely.

This early-access version may change as the product develops. Before a public commercial launch, this policy should be reviewed for the jurisdictions and services actually used.
